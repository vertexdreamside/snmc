Nurse & Midwife Portal Guide

Sign in, keep your details up to date, and take part in Council elections. Each section shows the page, then the steps.

01

Signing in

Go to snmc.databytes.sc and choose the Nurse / Midwife login.

  1. Type your Registration Number exactly as it appears on your certificate.
  2. If the Council has your National ID Number (NIN) on file, type it in the second box. Type it as it appears on your ID, including the hyphen.
  3. Choose Sign In.
Could not sign in?The message is always "We couldn't verify those details". This is deliberate, so nobody can guess who is registered. Check your number and NIN, then contact the Council office (Section 11).

There is no password. Use Logout at the top right when you finish, especially on a shared device.

02

Your Home page

Home is the first page after sign-in. It works on a phone as well as a computer.

  • The top menu has Home, Profile and Results, plus your name and Logout.
  • Your Profile shows your registration status, your category (Nurse, Midwife or Both) and your profile status. Choose View / Edit full details to open the full profile.
  • When an election is running, a box appears below with a link to nominate (Round 1) or to vote (Round 2). Otherwise it says nothing needs your attention.
  • If someone has nominated you, a banner says You've been nominated with buttons to accept or decline (Section 8).
03

Your profile

Open it with Profile in the top menu. The top of the page shows two things.

  • A status banner, such as "Profile Up to Date", so you can see at a glance if a change is waiting for Council review.
  • Registration (set by the Council): your Nurse and Midwife registration numbers, category, registration status and profile status. You cannot edit these. If one is wrong, contact the Council office.

Below that are three request cards (Licence Renewal, Name Change, Email Address) and the six-step details form.

Nothing changes straight awayAnything you submit goes to the Council for review. Your official record only changes once an administrator approves it.
04

Updating your details

The details form has six steps. A bar above the form shows where you are. Use Next and Back to move. Boxes marked with a red star are required, and Next stays greyed out until they are filled.

  1. Personal: first and last name, sex, date of birth, NIN and nationality. Your current NIN is hidden for privacy. Leave the box blank to keep it, or type a new one to change it.
  2. Contact: your address (line 1 required), mobile number (required) and home number.
  3. Professional: employer and place of work (both required), employment sector, service category (Hospital, Community or Private) and training institute.
  4. Licence: your licence numbers and expiry dates, locked and for reading only. Use the renewal request to change an expiry date (Section 5).
  5. Special Licences: extra certifications. Use Add Special Licence to add one. It goes to the Council for review before it is added to your record.
  6. Review: a summary of everything, each with an Edit link. Add an optional reason for the change, then submit.
Your service category mattersIt decides who you can vote for and who can vote for you (Section 9). Keep it correct.
05

Licence renewal

When you renew your licence, tell the Council so your record shows the new expiry date.

  1. On the Profile page, next to Licence Renewal, choose Request Renewal.
  2. Enter the new expiry date from your renewed certificate.
  3. Submit the request.

Your official expiry date only changes once the Council approves the request. Choose Cancel to close the form without sending.

06

Name change

Use this if your legal name has changed. The Council must see proof first, so your name does not change until they approve.

  1. On the Profile page, next to Name Change Request, choose Request Name Change.
  2. Type your new first name and new last name.
  3. Choose the reason: Marriage, Divorce, Legal Change of Name or Other. Add notes if helpful.
  4. Attach a supporting document, such as a marriage certificate or legal document.
  5. Choose Submit Request.
07

Email address

The Email Address card on the Profile page shows the email the Council holds for you, or "No email address on file". Choose Add Email (or Update if you already have one) to save your address. An email on file lets the Council reach you about your registration and elections.

08

Nominations (Round 1)

Screenshots to followThis section and the next two will get screenshots after the next test election.

When nominations are open, Home shows a Nominations Open — Round 1 box. Choose the election to open the nomination form.

Who can nominate whom

  • A registered nurse-midwife (both registrations) may nominate a Nurse and a Midwife.
  • A registered nurse (only) may nominate a Nurse.
  • A midwife who is not also a nurse may nominate a Midwife.

Making a nomination

  1. Start typing the person's name in Name (search the register) and pick them from the list.
  2. Enter their current placement.
  3. Choose the sector they work in: Hospital, Community or Private. These are separate groups and decide who can vote for them.
  4. Submit. A confirmation appears.
One nomination per categoryYou cannot submit another nomination in the same round once you have sent one.

If you are nominated

A banner on Home says You've been nominated, showing the category and election. Choose Accept to go forward to the Round 2 ballot, or Decline. Only accepted candidates appear on the ballot.

09

Voting (Round 2)

When voting is open, Home shows a Voting Open — Round 2 box. Choose the election to see your ballot.

  1. The ballot lists the candidates who accepted their nomination, in a box for each category you can vote in: Registered NURSE and/or Registered MIDWIFE.
  2. Tick one candidate in each category.
  3. Confirm your vote for that category. You see "Your vote has been recorded".
  4. Write down your reference number. It proves you voted. It does not reveal your choice to anyone, including the Council.
  • You get one vote per category, and it cannot be changed once recorded.
  • You can vote for candidates in your own sector (Hospital, Community or Private). If your sector is not on file, you can vote for any candidate.
  • Your vote is anonymous.
Cannot vote?Messages you may see: "Your Nurse/Midwife category hasn't been confirmed by the Council yet", or voting has not opened or has closed. Contact the Council office if you think this is wrong.
10

Election results

Choose Results in the top menu. Once the Council publishes an election, the winners appear here, listed by name and category (Nurse or Midwife). Results are never shown while voting is still open.

11

Getting help

Contact the Council office for anything this guide cannot fix: a wrong registration number, a category not confirmed, or sign-in problems.
